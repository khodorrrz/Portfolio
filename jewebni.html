Jewebni.

Sync · Full-stack developer intern

From a business’s catalog to a customer order

At Sync, I worked on Jewebni, an AI-assisted platform for customer conversations, ordering, and merchant operations. My work included order and inventory workflows, pricing, returns, voice integration, and delivery calculations.

The customer experience

How an order moves through Jewebni

  1. Import business knowledge

    Menus, documents, and website content populate the merchant’s catalog and knowledge base.

  2. Resolve the request

    The assistant reads business-scoped information and calls tools for menu items, availability, and delivery zones.

  3. Confirm and hand off

    Order tools carry confirmed details into merchant operations, where staff can track fulfillment.

The repository has a NestJS API, a merchant console, and a separate customer-facing Next.js app. The assistant uses application tools for live business data such as menus, delivery zones, and order status.
