HandyLink.

Marketplace · In development

A marketplace for finding and booking local service workers

I’m developing HandyLink as a marketplace project. It brings worker discovery, job posts, bids, bookings, messaging, and verification into one platform. I also worked on the automated AI workflow for triage and specialist matching.

Across the system

  1. Marketplace

    A bilingual Next.js interface covers discovery, job posts, bookings, and conversations.

  2. Transactions and trust

    A NestJS API with PostgreSQL manages marketplace state, including rescheduling, disputes, and identity-review workflows.

  3. AI services

    A separate FastAPI service supports chat, recommendations, price estimates, and advisory fraud signals.
