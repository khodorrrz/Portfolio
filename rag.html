From indexed PDF to cited answer

Architecture walkthrough

01Index

A Redis-backed worker parses and embeds documents.

02Retrieve

Combine keyword and vector results within the user's document scope.

03Rerank

Score the candidate passages and apply the threshold.

04Cite

Build the answer from retrieved evidence and attach backend-controlled citations.

Engineering notes

The indexer parses and chunks PDFs, then stores embeddings in pgvector. At query time, hybrid search and reranking select evidence from the authenticated user's documents. Backend-controlled citations tie answers to retrieved passages.
